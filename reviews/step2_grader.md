## Findings

MAJOR — reviews/step2_analysis.md ("The three errors" items 1–2 and "What the model is good and bad at"); ISSUES.md entry "[Step 2] v1 prompt policy weaknesses exposed by the 3 errors" — The analysis says errors 17 and 46 happened because the prompt's own rules pushed the model to NEGATIVE. The prompt text says the opposite: for both reviews it points to POSITIVE. So these errors show the model not following its instructions, not flawed rules. That makes the answer to "what is the model bad at?" wrong about the cause. It also risks a later prompt revision "fixing" rules that are not broken. — Evidence: the prompt says `Use the title only when the text is too short or vague to decide on its own.` and `Text that adds nothing beyond the title (a repeat of the title, a placeholder such as ".", or filler): classify from the title.` Review 46 is title "Love it!!" with text "This is an online gift card nothing to show sorry🤪". That text is short filler, so the rules say classify from the title, which gives POSITIVE. The analysis instead says "The prompt says the TEXT decides ... This exposes a flaw in the v1 title/text rule." For mixed reviews the prompt says `If they could not use the card, lost money, or say they would not buy again, pick NEGATIVE. If the problem was minor and they are satisfied overall, pick POSITIVE.` Review 17 ("the recipient loved it ! I have one complaint ... the note wasn't attached") meets none of the NEGATIVE conditions, so the rules give POSITIVE. The analysis instead says "The prompt's mixed-review rule weighs the complaint ... A policy disagreement, not a reading failure." Both saved raw responses are `LABEL=NEGATIVE`. — Suggested fix: Describe 17 and 46 as cases where the model did not follow the prompt: it ignored the filler-text rule when a positive title met a joking or apologetic text, and it let one non-blocking complaint outweigh an explicit "recipient loved it". Say they are not rule gaps. Rewrite the ISSUES.md entry to match. Only if a later prompt version is planned, note that the fix is stronger instructions or examples, not changed rules.
